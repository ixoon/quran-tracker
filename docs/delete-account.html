Delete your Quran Tracker account

Quran Tracker lets you create an optional account for sign-in. You can delete your account and associated cloud authentication data at any time.

Option 1: Delete in the app (recommended)

  1. Open Quran Tracker on your device.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm the prompt. If you signed up with email and password, enter your password.

This permanently deletes your account and cloud authentication data. This cannot be undone.

Option 2: Request deletion by email

If you no longer have the app installed, email us from the address linked to your account:

Email: webdevelopment492@gmail.com

Use the subject line Delete my Quran Tracker account.

We will delete your account and associated cloud authentication data within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account email and authentication identifiers stored with Supabase
  • Google account link (if you used Google Sign-In)

What is not automatically deleted

Reading progress, favorites, goals, prayer settings, and other data stored locally on your device are not automatically removed when you delete your account.

To remove local data, uninstall the app or clear Quran Tracker storage in your device settings.
